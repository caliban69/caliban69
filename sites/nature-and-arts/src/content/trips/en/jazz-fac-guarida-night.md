The evening opens with a private Latin jazz keyboard class — a working
musician walks you through the Afro-Cuban rhythms and harmonic language
that shape the island's music, in a one-on-one or small-group setting
rather than a stage performance.

From there we head to Fábrica de Arte Cubano (FAC), housed inside a
former cooking-oil factory in Vedado built in 1910. Opened in 2014 under
the direction of musician X Alfonso, FAC blends visual art, live music,
film, dance, design, and fashion under one roof, and was named one of
Time Magazine's 100 Best Places in the World in 2019 — it's as much a
night out as it is a gallery visit.

The night closes with a gala dinner at La Guarida, Havana's most
celebrated paladar since Enrique and Odeisys Núñez opened it in 1996
inside a crumbling early-20th-century mansion in Centro Habana. The
restaurant found fame as the filming location for the Oscar-nominated
*Fresa y Chocolate*, and has hosted guests from Madonna to Beyoncé —
reservations for a group dinner here need to be arranged well ahead of
time.
