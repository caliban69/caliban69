Cuba holds more endemic bird species relative to its size than almost
anywhere in the Western Hemisphere — a product of its long isolation and
range of habitats, from coastal wetlands to montane forest. This itinerary
is built to find them methodically, with the kind of field pacing a PhD
ornithologist uses in their own research, not a tour built purely around
photo stops.
