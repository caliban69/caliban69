Escape the crowds and step into a world of biodiversity and vibrant
feathers. This half-day nature expedition is designed for those who
appreciate the beauty of the wild at a relaxed, enjoyable pace — gentle
trails, no expertise required, and a finish before midday so your
afternoon stays free for the beach or the city.

Within just a few hours we introduce you to the "Big Three" of the Cuban
forest: the Cuban Trogon, dressed in the colors of the Cuban flag; the
tiny, jewel-toned Cuban Tody, a favorite for every photographer; and the
Cuban Solitaire, famous for its flute-like song echoing through the trees
— alongside some of Cuba's flagship landscapes, from karst formations to
mangrove.

High-quality binoculars are provided and our guide helps you spot birds
instantly and frame the best angles for photos. No experience? No problem
— bring your camera and your sense of wonder.
