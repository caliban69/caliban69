This full-day expedition is meticulously designed to target the most
sought-after species within reach of Havana. We journey through a mosaic
of diverse habitats — from ancient karst forests (mogotes) to lush
mangrove systems, finishing with an afternoon push into the wetlands for
shorebirds — offering a masterclass in Cuban biodiversity.

On this journey we seek approximately half of the island's endemics and
several regional Caribbean endemics, plus wintering Nearctic species. This
is not a casual walk in the woods — it's a dedicated mission for the
serious birder, built around habitat precision and acoustic tracking to
secure your lifers, while still accommodating your pace.

We drive out early to capture the peak of the morning chorus. Our guide
tracks your personal target list throughout the day to prioritize the
sightings you need most, and shares a comprehensive eBird or spreadsheet
list of everything seen at the end of the tour.

**Meet Freddy.** With over 30 years traversing the island's wildest
corners, Freddy is a prominent figure in the local birding community.
Beyond guiding, he mentors the next generation of professionals at the
University of Havana's Faculty of Tourism, and treats every outing as a
chance to inspire others to protect Cuba's natural heritage.
