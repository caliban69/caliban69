endemics found nowhere else on the island, and one of the Caribbean's most
intact reptile and amphibian communities, concentrated in the Guanahacabibes
Peninsula's UNESCO biosphere reserve. This trip is built for travelers who want
their binoculars and their headlamps in equal use.
